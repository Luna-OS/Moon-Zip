The first Moon Zip: an archive manager for Windows in the Moon night-sky look, with the real 7-Zip 26.03 inside.

## Download
- **Moon-Zip-Setup-0.1.0.exe**: installer. Installs per user, no admin rights needed, and offers to add Moon Zip to the right-click menu.
- **Moon-Zip-0.1.0-win-x64.zip**: portable version. Unzip it and run `Moon Zip.exe`.

The builds are not code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen warns the first time you start them. Click "More info" and then "Run anyway".

## What's in it
- **Open** 7z, ZIP, RAR, TAR, GZ, XZ, BZ2, ZST, ISO, CAB, WIM, DMG, DEB, RPM and 40 more formats, split archives included. Archives inside archives open with a double-click.
- **Create** 7z, ZIP, tar, tar.gz, tar.xz and tar.bz2: six compression levels, the method of your choice, AES-256 passwords (7z can hide the file names too) and splitting into volumes.
- **Change** 7z, ZIP, tar and WIM archives in place: add files (or drop them on the window), rename and delete.
- **Extract** everything or just the selection, into a new folder named after the archive or anywhere else, and choose what happens to files that exist already.
- **Test** archives, and work out **checksums** (CRC-32, MD5, SHA-1, SHA-256, SHA-512, BLAKE2b) with a field to compare against.
- **Find** items anywhere in an archive as you type.
- **Right-click menu** in Windows Explorer: "Extract with Moon Zip" (Open, Extract here, Extract to new folder, Extract to…, Test archive) and "Compress with Moon Zip" (Add to archive…, Compress to .7z, Compress to .zip, Checksums…). Several selected files are handled together.
- **Night and day** themes, or follow Windows.
- **The installer** is in the Moon look too: night-sky welcome and finish pages, the logo in the page header.
